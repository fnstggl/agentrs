## CAM LIVE — COMPLETE THE CORE INTELLIGENCE

The current CAM Live implementation appears substantially built, but several central capabilities remain incomplete according to the integration documentation, scientific results and known-failures register.
The original CAM Live specification remains authoritative. This amendment prioritizes the remaining shortcomings that most directly constrain CAM's ability to acquire generalizable intelligence through experience.
Independently audit the current implementation of each capability below, investigate its fundamental shortcomings, and complete or improve it as necessary.

### 1. Genuinely learned rewards
The reward network receives meta-updates, but the bootstrap mixture remains at 1.0, preventing learned rewards from influencing ordinary policy updates under the default configuration.
This undermines one of CAM's central architectural principles: learning what deserves reinforcement through experience.
Investigate and resolve the underlying causes, including:

* The scarcity of independently verified outcome groups.
* The current dependence on completed objective lifetimes.
* Meta-gradient effectiveness under realistic experience availability.
* The reward promotion and bootstrap transition criteria.
* Whether learned rewards meaningfully influence subsequent behavior.

CAM must be capable of learning its internal reward function during a single extended real-world objective as sufficient independently grounded evidence accumulates, rather than requiring many completed objectives before meaningful reward learning begins.
Do not simply force the bootstrap mixture lower to demonstrate that the reward network affects behavior. Establish an empirically grounded mechanism for determining when learned rewards are useful.
Demonstrate the complete pathway from experience to reward learning to changed policy updates and subsequent behavior.

### 2. Predictive imagination and multistep planning
Imagined future values are computed but are not currently used by action selection.
Complete the integration of predictive imagination into actual decision-making.
CAM should be capable of internally considering multiple possible sequences of actions, anticipating their consequences, comparing alternative approaches, identifying useful experiments and selecting actions based on expected long-term outcomes and uncertainty.
This should extend beyond evaluating isolated immediate actions.
Investigate how the learned latent world model can support increasingly sophisticated planning as CAM accumulates experience.
Demonstrate that imagined future consequences causally influence actual decisions, while preventing unsupported or poorly calibrated predictions from dominating action selection.

### 3. Persistent internal representations
Investigate why every experiential core call passes `state=None`, rather than preserving an evolving latent state across decisions.
Determine whether the current re-encoding of recent context and fast beliefs adequately satisfies the original architectural requirements.
If not, implement persistent learned internal representations that evolve through experience and support long-horizon understanding, prediction and decision-making.
Evaluate whether the resulting representation improves learning, retention and understanding of unfamiliar situations.
Do not introduce recurrence merely for architectural complexity. Its contribution must be meaningful.

### 4. Retrospective credit assignment
RUDDER and COCOA exist but are not the active default credit estimators.
Investigate why they failed their held-out selection criteria and improve CAM's ability to retrospectively understand which earlier decisions contributed to later outcomes.
CAM should be capable of revisiting experiences when new information becomes available, revising its earlier causal understanding and updating its neural weights accordingly.
Retain scientifically justified estimator selection. Do not force a particular algorithm to be the default simply because it was named in the specification.
The fundamental requirement is effective learning from delayed, uncertain and interconnected consequences.

### 5. Autonomous strategic invention
The real-model planning rehearsals produced no substantive research notes, hypotheses or plans.
Investigate why CAM is not independently researching, hypothesizing, planning and composing novel long-horizon strategies effectively.
Examine the actual action-generation architecture, semantic conditioning, candidate selection, predictive evaluation, exploration mechanisms, context management and learning dynamics.
CAM must be capable of:

* Understanding unfamiliar situations.
* Identifying potentially valuable opportunities.
* Generating novel approaches.
* Inventing useful software, resources and interventions.
* Independently designing informative experiments.
* Sustaining coherent strategies across many decisions.
* Revising or abandoning strategies when evidence contradicts them.

Do not solve this by hardcoding strategic workflows, supplying domain-specific strategies or substituting a conventional prompted LLM planner for CAM's experiential intelligence.
The objective is for strategic competence to emerge and improve through the integrated neural learning architecture.

### 6. Autonomous skill acquisition
Demonstrate actual acquisition, validation and subsequent reuse of independently discovered executable skills.
CAM should recognize useful recurring procedures, convert them into reusable capabilities, determine when they are applicable and improve its execution efficiency through experience.
Verify that acquired skills can be composed into more sophisticated actions and reused in unfamiliar situations.
Distinguish procedural skill accumulation from persistent neural intelligence acquisition. Both are valuable, but the former cannot substitute for the latter.

### 7. Goal-free learning
Complete CAM's ability to independently discover useful learning objectives without an externally assigned task.
CAM should identify gaps in its understanding, select informative experiences, investigate unfamiliar mechanisms and acquire capabilities that may improve its performance on future objectives.
Replace placeholder or constant estimates of transferable competence with meaningful learned estimates where required.
Evaluate whether self-directed experience produces measurable improvements on subsequently assigned, unfamiliar objectives.

### 8. Verification and scientific standards
Do not merely demonstrate that each component exists. Demonstrate that each participates meaningfully in the integrated intelligence.
Use the actual full-sized CAM Live architecture for substantive evaluations, including the pretrained backbone, experiential core, semantic adapters and open-ended action-generation capabilities.
Retain smaller models and numerical environments only for bounded engineering diagnostics.
For each capability, distinguish:

* Implementation correctness.
* Integration into the actual runtime.
* Causal influence on behavior.
* Measured improvement in learning or competence.

Use independent verification and appropriate ablations to establish these distinctions.
Do not manufacture positive scientific findings. Investigate negative results and distinguish architectural defects from genuine empirical limitations.

Continue preparing and conducting the authorized Discord planning experiment in parallel with these improvements, subject to deployment preflight and existing authorization boundaries.
