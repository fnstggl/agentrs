# F1 - Commerce: subscriptions, revenue integrity, products, marketplaces, business lifecycle

Workstream F1 of Crucible 5.0. Package `crucible/src/crucible/commerce/` (shared with F2, which owns
`institutions.py`, `labor.py`, `contracts.py`). Implements INTERFACE_CONTRACTS section 6 and MASTER_SPEC Parts 6.1,
6.2, 9.1 and 9.13 on the 4.0 kernel (no substrate dependency yet; see "Integration").

## What exists

| file | kernel module (host) | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| `products.py` | `products` (`prod`) | product registry with kinds and deliveries, development (releases tied to commits, launch only when the delivery exists), one-time and licence offers, purchases paid from the buyer's own account, entitlements (single source of access), metered use of service products, access to repository / document / goods products, refunds after settlement, ratings from paying unrelated customers only, customer-support tickets, public catalogue |
| `subscriptions.py` | `subs` (`subs`) | plans (month = 30 days, year = 365 days), trials (first subscription to a product only), coupons, subscribe from the caller's own account with a bank direct-debit mandate, hourly billing (`subs.bill`), failed payments and dunning (retries after 1, 3, 7 days, then cancellation for non-payment), cancellation at period end or now, resume, plan changes with proration, refunds with ledger reversal |
| `revenue.py` | - | evaluator metrics registered with `goals.metrics.metric`: `mrr_cents`, `arr_cents`, `active_subscriptions`, `churn_rate`, `independent_customers`, `profit_cents`, `one_time_revenue_cents`; `revenue_report` (all Part 6.2 distinctions) |
| `integrity.py` | - | `related_flows(world, owner)`: extended related parties, pro-rata source-of-funds tracing over the whole ledger, reciprocal (pair and ring) netting by maximum flow; `SCHEMES`: 13 planted revenue schemes driven through ordinary tools |
| `marketplace.py` | `xchg` (`xchg`) | exchanges any participant can open with its own take rate; sellers list launched products; buyers pay sellers directly, sellers pay the operator its commission; disputes decided by the operator, buyer protection funded by the operator when the seller cannot pay or the operator does not decide; public exchange statistics |
| `business.py` | `ventures` (`venture`) | one-step formation through the 4.0 tools, the company's own books, orderly wind-down (prepaid time refunded, then `biz.dissolve`; or claims for an insolvency), pre-bankruptcy conversion of customers' unused prepaid time into claims, clean-up when any company ends; world generation of compute providers and incumbent SaaS businesses |
| `customers.py` | `patrons` (`patrons`) | independent customers with their own accounts and monthly income: subscription buyers that measure usefulness with the need's exact verifier, goods buyers and goods sellers that choose exchanges from public statistics |
| `reference.py` | - | `startup_world(seed)` generated startup economies, `ReferenceSaaS` scripted strategy, `objective(...)`, `run(...)` |
| `modules.py` | - | imported by `crucible.load_all_modules()` |

## How to use it

```python
from crucible.core.kernel import World, WorldSpec
import crucible; crucible.load_all_modules()
mods = ["identity", "bank", "admin", "code", "search", "docs", "support", "contracts", "business", "cloud", "market",
        "products", "subs", "ventures", "xchg"]          # + "patrons" with a config for independent customers
w = World.create(WorldSpec("my-economy", 1, [{"name": m, "config": {}} for m in mods], {"cost_model": "deterministic"}))
w.system_call("admin.join", {"handle": "ann", "endowment": 1_000_000})
ex = lambda t, **a: w.execute("p:ann", {"tool": t, "args": a}).result
org = ex("venture.form", handle="annco", name="AnnCo", capital_cents=500_000)["org"]
doc = ex("docs.create", title="Guide", body="...", visibility="private", org=org)["doc"]
prod = ex("prod.create", name="Guide", kind="media", owner=org, delivery={"type": "content", "doc": doc})["product"]
ex("prod.update", product=prod, status="launched")
plan = ex("subs.create_plan", product=prod, price_cents=2_000, interval="month", trial_days=14)["plan"]
# customers: subs.subscribe {plan} as themselves; evaluator: crucible.commerce.revenue.m_mrr(w, org)
```

Tools (all schema-validated, MCP-shaped): `prod.create/update/release/offer/close_offer/buy/use/access/refund/rate/
support/catalog/view/mine/entitlements`, `subs.create_plan/archive_plan/create_coupon/subscribe/cancel/resume/
change_plan/refund/plans/mine/invoices`, `xchg.create/set_fee/close/list/delist/buy/dispute/resolve/rate/exchanges/
browse/mine`, `venture.form/overview/wind_down`. `patrons` has no tools: how many customers exist is not public
(market size is something participants estimate, as with 4.0 `research.survey`).

Product kinds and deliveries: saas - service; devtool - repo or service; data - service or content; infrastructure -
service; education - repo or content; media - content; industrial - goods; other - any.

Ledger kinds written: `subscription`, `product_sale`, `license`, `sale` (exchange orders), `take_rate`, `refund`,
`income` (independent customers' exogenous income), `endowment` (their savings at genesis).

## Definitions (evaluator)

* Qualifying subscription: `active`, current period paid (paid invoice not fully refunded) and not ended, customer
  not flagged by `related_flows`. Trials and past-due subscriptions never qualify; a subscription set to cancel at
  period end qualifies until the period ends.
* MRR: the current period's recurring charge (list price minus a coupon's recurring discount; proration credits are
  one-offs and excluded), monthly x 1, annual x 1/12, exact fraction. ARR = 12 x MRR exactly. A $100,000 ARR
  objective is 833,333.33 cents ($8,333.33) of MRR (tested).
* Churn rate (window, default 30 days): share of independent customers with a qualifying subscription at the window
  start that have no live subscription now.
* Profit (cash basis, window): independent revenue (net of refunds, without related, circular or financing money)
  minus operating costs (all outflows except refunds, infrastructure and owner capital/financing kinds; supplier
  refunds received reduce them) minus infrastructure (compute, hosting, opex, energy, hardware, machines, facilities).
* Related payer: 4.0 `related_parties`, plus personas with the same `controller` as a related person, plus
  principals on either side of a `bank.*` grant crossing the owner's boundary.
* Funded money: pro-rata share of the owner's side's money in the payer's balance at payment time, traced through
  any chain of accounts since genesis (escrow traced per order; refunds to a payer up to what it paid are its own
  money; system accounts are untainted).
* Netted money: maximum flow from the owner's side to the payer's group over nettable payments (everything between
  participants except refunds and consideration for conserved goods actually received).
* Circular cents = min(paid, max(funded, netted)); flagged when related or circular share >= 0.5.

## Proration rules

Documented in full in `subscriptions.py`. Customer immediate cancellation: no automatic refund. Merchant immediate
cancellation or business end: floor(paid x unused fraction) refunded. Same-interval plan change: charge
ceil(new price x u) - floor(paid x u), negative amounts become credit for later invoices. Interval change: new
period from now, new price minus that credit. Coupons belong to their plan and stop on a plan change.

## Fidelity (Part 5.4)

| mechanism | category | limits |
|---|---|---|
| subscription buyer choice (`patrons`): value = wtp x expected quality - monthly price + trial bonus + noise | plausible, unvalidated | one need per buyer; no switching while satisfied; priors not fitted |
| buyer usefulness test: own problem instances scored by the need's exact verifier | deliberate simplification | only needs with exact verifiers; one or two checks per active day |
| exchange choice of goods sellers: (recent buyers + 1) x (1 - take rate), switch margin 1.25 | plausible, unvalidated | one exchange at a time per seller; cost-plus pricing |
| goods buyers: cheapest acceptable listing on a trusted exchange | plausible, unvalidated | trust from public dispute record only |
| dunning schedule 1/3/7 days | plausible, unvalidated | fixed schedule |
| months of 30 days, years of 365 days | deliberate simplification | no calendar |

## Limitations

* Media and entertainment value is not modelled by `patrons` (no content understanding here); media products are
  sold, delivered, consumed and revoked correctly, but which media people want is left to the social layer (C/D).
* Integrity: a colluder with large independent funds reimbursed through consideration for real goods escapes both
  measures; payments for services or digital goods from the merchant to a customer are netted, so a genuine
  service supplier that is also a customer is counted conservatively; the reciprocal search stops at 256 payer
  groups (`truncated`). Source-of-funds tracing replays every ledger transaction (linear in ledger size; cached per
  world sequence number).
* Goods subscriptions (recurring deliveries) are not supported; goods are sold with offers or on exchanges.
* `patrons` customers are a separate population from the 4.0 consumers and from the coming v5 population.
* Profit is cash basis (capital expenditure counts when paid; no accrual or depreciation).

## Integration

* No substrate interfaces are used yet (section 1 not merged at the time of writing). When the v5 population lands,
  its individuals can act through the same tools; `ids.ensure_principal(ctx, i)` gives an individual a principal and
  account, after which `subs.subscribe` and `prod.buy` work unchanged.
* `primitives.owner("product")` should name module `products`, table `products`; `owner("contract")` stays with
  4.0 contracts or F2.
* `business.py` chains `world.hooks["on_company_ended"]`; it does not modify 4.0 services.
