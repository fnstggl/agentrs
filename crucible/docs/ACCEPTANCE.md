# Crucible 4.0 acceptance tests

Generated by `examples/run_acceptance.py`. The result column is what pytest reported for that test. Measured numbers are in the linked JSON record; caveats are part of the result.

| test | title | result | time | record | main caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Real software creation | **PASSED** | 35 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/A_real_software_creation.json) | The builder is a scripted control that knows one piece of developer craft (inducing a record wire format from worked examples); it shows the world supports the path, not that a learner finds it. |
| B | Multiple viable strategies | **PASSED** | 327 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/B_multiple_viable_strategies.json) | Both strategies are carried out by scripted competent controls that decide from what they observe. This shows the world admits more than one route to the same verified outcome; it does not show that a learner finds either. |
| C | Unknown mechanism discovery | **PASSED** | 20 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/C_unknown_mechanism_discovery.json) | The operator's procedure (one lever at a time from the defaults, then commit) is uninformative on the technical-systems engine, where levers interact and take effect with a lag: there the informed and deprived operators both do worse than leaving the defaults alone. The claim is established for the causal-model families only. |
| D | Structural transfer | **PASSED** | 173 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/D_structural_transfer.json) | This test establishes that the protocol measures structural transfer on equal experience; it asserts no particular result. With this small budget and n=6 the measured difference is reported as it came out. |
| E | Autonomous competition | **PASSED** | 114 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/E_autonomous_competition.json) | The learners are stochastic-approximation controllers on a few policy parameters; opponents in the 2x2 are scripted profiles. Richer adaptation in joint training is reported from the recorded self-play experiment, with its uncertainty. |
| F | Cooperation | **PASSED** | 84 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/F_cooperation.json) | Three world instances; a paired interval over three is reported but flagged low-n. 'Cooperation' here is two independent learners sharing a team objective with no communication channel; each sees only its own gauges. |
| G | Invention and second-order effects | **PASSED** | 292 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/G_invention_and_second_order_effects.json) | The inventor is a scripted competent control; the firms that adopt, the entrepreneurs who imitate and the providers that reprice are rule-based minds. The chain shows what the world's mechanics support. It is a controlled simulated counterfactual, not evidence about a real economy. |
| H | Long-term sustainability | **PASSED** | 219 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/H_long_term_sustainability.json) | The in-test run is 28 simulated days; the 90-, 180- and 365-day runs are executed separately (examples/long_run4.py) and their recorded results are checked here when present. |
| I | Genuine customer value | **PASSED** | 22 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/I_genuine_customer_value.json) | Customers are simulated: they refuse answers whose verified score is below a floor and rate from experienced quality. That real customers behave this way is a modelling assumption. |
| J | Reward-hacking resistance | **PASSED** | 70 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/J_reward_hacking_resistance.json) | A probe that holds shows that one concrete attack fails, not that no attack exists. One finding stays open (a rewritten event-log suffix needs an anchor kept outside the store; it requires write access to the store, which no in-world participant has). Remaining weaker variants (colluders trading across the netting window, a paid one-cent order buying a rating) are listed in the limitations. |
| K | Architecture-neutral evaluation | **PASSED** | 74 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/K_architecture_neutral_evaluation.json) | Language-model agents use the same world through the text tool interface (agents/llm.py) rather than this numeric contract; both reach the facility through the same `rig.*` tools. |
| L | Parametric learning | **PASSED** | 231 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/L_parametric_learning.json) | Shown on easy hand-made mechanisms from one structural family, where learning is cheap. On the real benchmark families trained PPO improved far less; see the learner table. |
| M | Reproducibility | **PASSED** | 32 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/M_reproducibility.json) | Guarantees: replay of recorded events is exact always; regeneration is exact because outcomes the kernel cannot recompute (program outputs, measured CPU) are recorded; fork-versus-parent equality and cross-process equality hold under the deterministic cost model only. With measured CPU cost, two fresh executions of the same world differ. A handler that keeps state in process memory can still cost more on a fresh sandbox worker. |
| N | Clean installation | **PASSED** | 66 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/N_clean_installation.json) | Installs the core and dev extras. The web, browser, PostgreSQL and RL extras, the Crucible sandbox's root requirement and model weights are documented separately and are not part of this check. |
| O | Independent generalization | **PASSED** | 45 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/O_independent_generalization.json) | These are test-split families at public validation seeds, so the procedure, not the numbers, is what this test establishes. The final figures come from one opening of the sealed set. |
| P | Security | **PASSED** | 6 s | [json](experiments/acceptance/P_security.json) | Isolation is an unprivileged uid with Landlock and seccomp inside an unprivileged container, not a VM: a host-kernel exploit through a permitted system call would escape it. All sandbox workers share one uid; signalling, resource limits and scheduling calls on other processes are filtered, the shared uid itself remains. |

Reproduce one test: `cd crucible && python -m pytest -q 'tests/test_acceptance.py::test_A_real_software_creation'`
(tests that run agent code need root, Landlock and seccomp).
